# Konstantinos Kitsios

**PhD Student in AI for Software Testing**

[Website](https://kitsiosk.github.io/) · [Publications](https://kitsiosk.github.io/publications) 

I'm a PhD student at the University of Zurich, working with [Alberto Bacchelli](https://sback.it/) in the [ZEST](https://www.ifi.uzh.ch/en/zest.html) group. My research brings together large language models, search-based testing, and fuzzing, with broader interests in software engineering and AI for code.

Previously, I worked at Tesla on distributed software systems and databases. I hold an integrated master's degree from Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, Greece.


## Impact of my Research in Open-Source Software
If you are interested in my publications, have a look at my [website](https://kitsiosk.github.io/) or [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=R3b_aQkAAAAJ). This section focuses on the impact of my research in open-source software.

Research tools are most useful when they make a difference in real projects. 
My research tools have generated tests that guard against regression bugs for
[Mozilla](https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/research/library/automated-generation-of-issue-reproducing-tests-by-combining-llms-and-search-based-testing/)
and optimized CI/CD in projects such as
[Azure Developer CLI](https://github.com/Azure/azure-dev/pull/5471).
In total, contributions generated by **BLAST** (ASE '25) and **LeaFlow** (under review) have resulted in
**42 merged pull requests** across 40 open-source repositories, listed below.
